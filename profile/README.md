## What we build

**Epiow** is the **organization operating system** for companies that run on real rules — starting with Hong Kong.

| | |
|--|--|
| **Platform OS** | Single workspace · modular app catalog · shared identity |
| **Workforce operations** | Leave · attendance · payroll · MPF / ORSO paths |
| **CRM & ops** | Role-based apps across the organization |
| **Intelligence** | Cross-app capability — structure first, not theatre |

> Not a US HR suite with a locale toggle. Local law is product.

---

## For whom

Founders, HR, and operations leaders in Hong Kong SMEs and mid-market teams who need **modern software** and **process they can defend** — plus implementers who want modular architecture without toy foundations.
